Welcome to Barbell Club

Barbell Club is a powerlifting and bodybuilding gym created for people who want to become stronger, build muscle, and improve their fitness.

High-Quality Equipment

Our gym provides power racks, competition benches, deadlift platforms, free weights, machines, and other equipment for powerlifting and bodybuilding.

Powerlifting Meets and Events

We host powerlifting meets, lifting events, and community activities throughout the year for lifters of all experience levels.

Our Community

Barbell Club has a supportive community where beginners and experienced lifters can train together and reach their goals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a serious but welcoming training environment with great equipment and a strong fitness community.
